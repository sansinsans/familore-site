Build, preserve, and explore your family tree.

Familore is a family tree app designed to help you collect names, photos, stories, and relationships in one calm, easy-to-read space.

The app focuses on clarity, local-first data handling, and a visual tree experience that works across desktop and mobile devices.

What Familore helps you do

Family tree building Create and edit people, partners, parents, and children in a visual tree layout.
Photo support Add profile photos to make family connections easier to remember.
Local-first storage Your tree data is designed to stay on your own device unless you choose to export it.
Import and backup Export your tree as JSON and restore it later when needed.
