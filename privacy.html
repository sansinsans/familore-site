Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 22, 2026

Familore is designed to let users create and manage family trees primarily on their own device. This policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

1. Information handled by the app

Familore may store the following information that users choose to enter:

  • Names, maiden names, birth information, gender, notes, and family relationships
  • User-added profile photos for family members
  • Locally stored backup and restore data generated by the user

2. How data is stored

Familore is currently designed to store family tree data locally on the user's device or browser storage. The app does not require account creation to use the core features.

Data entered into the app is intended to remain on the user's own device unless the user explicitly exports it.

3. Data sharing

Familore does not sell personal data and does not intentionally share user-entered family tree information with third parties.

4. Analytics, ads, and tracking

Familore does not currently require user sign-in for the main experience and is not designed around advertising or third-party analytics for core family tree use.

5. User control

Users can control their information by:

  • Editing or deleting family members directly in the app
  • Exporting their data as a backup file
  • Removing locally stored data from their device or browser
